# Changes in this fork

This fork of [rev.ng](https://rev.ng/), a binary lifter and decompiler built on [LLVM](https://llvm.org/), makes rev.ng's whole pipeline (lifting, ABI detection, data layout analysis and C emission) complete on binaries with over 100,000 functions, and fixes wrong or crashing output it found on the way. [FORK.md](FORK.md) states the fork's goals. This page lists every change since the fork branched from rev.ng's `develop` at [`1e33c3359`](https://github.com/revng/revng/commit/1e33c335935ba43286017760ff396112914d4f6b) on 2026-09-16, grouped by what it fixes, with links to the commits; each commit message gives the problem, the mechanism and its evidence, and most correctness fixes come with a small [FileCheck](https://llvm.org/docs/CommandGuide/FileCheck.html) test under `tests/filecheck/` that fails without the change.

Branch `fix/emitted-c-fidelity` holds the whole current line; the few commits marked as on a side branch are on their own branches of this repository, and their links open them.

## How the changes are rated

Each change has two estimates, out of 100, of its prospects for upstream rev.ng.

Interest is how much the change serves rev.ng's stated use, decompilation for analysis of ordinary binaries:

- 85 to 100: wrong decompiled output, or a crash, on small ordinary input.
- 60 to 84: crashes or wrong results that need unusual input, determinism, or a speedup that also shows on mid-size binaries.
- 30 to 59: scale work that matters mainly for very large binaries, or recompilation.
- below 30: fork infrastructure, diagnostics or experiments.

Proof is how cheaply a reviewer can be convinced the change is correct:

- 85 to 100: a small assembly or IR test fails before and passes after, and the diff is short.
- 60 to 84: a unit test, or a test plus an argument about a heuristic.
- 30 to 59: needs output equality on public binaries, or a longer argument.
- below 30: shows only on very large inputs, or changes results in ways that need judgment.

Kind says what a change fixes: correctness (wrong or crashing output, with the narrower cases of recompilation, type quality and termination noted), determinism, performance, capacity (inputs that could not be stored at all), or diagnostics, infrastructure and cleanup. Stage names the pipeline step the change touches. Abbreviations: CSV is a CPU-state variable, rev.ng's model of a register or other piece of CPU state; DLA is data layout analysis; the Clifter converts LLVM IR to Clift, rev.ng's [MLIR](https://mlir.llvm.org/) dialect for C; pypeline is rev.ng's pipeline and cache framework.

## Patches to rev.ng's dependencies

The fork's builds also use patches to [LLVM](https://github.com/revng/llvm-project) and to libtcg, the instruction translator rev.ng takes from its [QEMU](https://github.com/revng/qemu) fork:
